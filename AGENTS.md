# Project workflow

- Install with `npm ci`; run the site with `npm run dev` and build with `npm run build`.
- `npm test` runs arithmetic, case-content, and case-progress tests using Node's built-in test runner.
- Frontend only: no backend, no accounts, no network calls. Every bit of progress lives in the browser's localStorage, and nothing is sent anywhere.
- `/` is the landing page (`src/Landing.jsx`) with "Open dashboard" calls. `/cases#…` is the app hub, with sections Overview, Case maths, Frameworks, Industries, Full cases, Mental maths, Cards, Interview, and Settings at `#overview`, `#case-maths`, `#frameworks`, `#industries`, `#full-cases`, `#mental-maths`, `#cards`, `#interview`, and `#settings`. `/brain`, `/cards`, and `/mental` keep working through client-side redirects to `/cases#mental-maths` and `/cases#cards`; `/about` redirects to `/`.

# Interface

- Editorial design system, shared by every page. All colours, radii, shadows, and fonts come from the tokens in `:root` of `src/styles.css`; add a token instead of a one-off hex value.
  - Type: Newsreader (serif) for headings, the `Case Vault` wordmark, and maths equations; IBM Plex Sans for text; IBM Plex Mono for timers and counters. Fonts are self-hosted through `@fontsource` packages imported in `src/main.jsx`.
  - Colour: warm paper background, deep ink text, one forest-green accent. Success, warning, and danger tints have their own tokens. No gradients or glows. The one exception is the landing page: its hero and closing call are dark `--night` panels with a faint grid and soft radial glow, and `--accent-bright` (mint) is the accent on those panels only.
  - Shape: `--radius` (4px) for controls, `--radius-card` (6px) for cards (inside the hub `src/app-skin.css` raises these to 10px and 18px, makes buttons pills, and adds the night Overview header), thin rules, and a 3px accent left rule for "next step" cards.
  - Labels are sentence case, never all caps. Icons come from `@phosphor-icons/react`, imported per icon (`@phosphor-icons/react/ArrowRight`) so unused icons stay out of the bundle.
- There is no sign-in. Mental maths rounds are generated in the browser (`createQuestion` in `src/arithmetic.js`); `src/MathPractice.jsx` keeps a personal best per settings combination in `case-prep-brain-best-v1`.
- Use system fonts, at least 44px interactive touch targets, and at least 16px editable inputs to avoid phone zoom. Check narrow layouts down to 320px.
- `src/ErrorBoundary.jsx` wraps the Landing and Cases chunks in `main.jsx`; a render error shows "Something went wrong." + Reload instead of a blank page.
- Every page shares one structure: a screen-reader-only h1 (the header nav names the page; no visible page titles or intro blurbs), fluid full-width landing layouts (no page cap; 24–40px gutters scaling with viewport) with `auto-fill` card grids, and focused reading screens centred at `--content-width` (880px on laptops). Main uses `src/AppShell.jsx`: a sticky left sidebar (sections, categories or decks, with counts) and an optional right filter rail on laptops; on phones the sidebar becomes the scroll-snapped tab row and filters fold into one `Filters` disclosure. Full cases list as searchable, sortable rows rather than cards.

# Case practice

- `src/casebook.js` contains six guided adaptations from the supplied IIMA 2020–21 casebook, converted from an earlier written format; `src/casebook-extra.js` adds further cases authored directly in tap form (no `LEGACY_CASES` entry), `src/casebook-2026.js` holds cases adapted from the IIMA 2026-27 prep book and the HBS MCC guide, and `src/casebook-wharton.js` holds cases adapted from the Wharton Consulting Club Casebook 2017. `CASES` concatenates all four. Write case text in Case Vault's own words (no 8+ word run matching a source); when source arithmetic does not reconcile, use labelled, self-consistent exercise assumptions. Do not adapt cases from commercial books (for example Robert Mellon's Case Interview Workbook) without the publisher's permission. Every case needs a matching `src/case-recall.js` entry. Keep provenance and page references in the data, but do not render credit bylines, “Adapted from” footers, or source-credit panels. Continue labelling added exercise assumptions.
- Follow Clarify → Structure → Calculate → Challenge → Recommend. Reveal worked answers and reference explanations only after an attempt.
- New Cases attempts are tap-only: select two priorities, reveal calculation choices after thinking, and build recommendations from action/evidence/risk choices. `src/case-decisions.js` supplies the options and case-specific feedback. No text inputs, uploads, or AI evaluation are needed.
- Decision checks assess the supplied scenario, not independent writing/speaking or consulting readiness. New attempts have eight checks; self-ratings never earn points or control their review scheduling.
- Preserve `LEGACY_CASES` for interpreting earlier responses. Progress v2 migrates from `case-prep-cases-v1` without overwriting that key. Submitted legacy writing stays ungraded and unfinished text stays read-only. Keep both prior completed reviews and migrated written drafts in `legacyReviews` when later attempts replace them; never discard one to preserve the other.
- `src/case-practice.js` manages local drafts, locked first attempts, optional pacing, and suggested 1/3/7/14-day recalls. Fully correct, unassisted tap-only attempts completed on or after the recall date advance the level; a clean replay before that date keeps the level and date unchanged. Mistakes, hints, or preserved legacy writing reset to level 0 and a one-day recall. Level 3 (`MASTERY_LEVEL`) shows as Mastered. The pace timer is not a deadline or score penalty.
- The Full cases library leads with one Next up card (`nextCase`: draft, then due recall, then new case), shows per-case status (`caseStatus`) and a three-step mastery meter, and ends with a Recall sheet. `src/case-recall.js` holds each case's takeaway, key number, common trap, and known facts; recall cards and the correct path (`winningPath`) unlock only after a case is finished once, so first attempts stay unspoiled.
- Quick recall (`src/recall-quiz.js`) unlocks after three finished cases: six tap questions (takeaway, key number, trap, opening question, best action) drawn only from finished cases, so unplayed cases are never spoiled. It is a warm-up and never changes mastery or recall dates.
- The review screen leads with the recall card, mastery and next recall date, then the correct path with the learner's misses marked; stage-by-stage feedback stays collapsed. Case play and review screens push a history entry so Back and the header Cases link return to the library.
- The Cases bundle is lazy-loaded and then kept mounted during navigation to retain unsaved in-memory progress when browser storage is unavailable. No case answers leave the browser.

# Framework practice

- `src/frameworks.js` holds 16 method guides and 42 drill families derived from the IIMA 2020-21 casebook, the IIMA 2026-27 prep book, and the HBS MCC guide, with provenance retained internally and no visible credit bylines. Each family has two contexts; eight numerical families generate fresh, checked figures. Keep examples labelled as practice assumptions rather than current market facts.
- The Framework drills section is lazy-loaded inside Cases. Keep it tap-only, with worked answers after a committed choice, optional method assistance, and links to related full cases.
- `src/framework-practice.js` stores a separate `case-prep-frameworks-v1` namespace. Never overwrite the case-progress v1/v2 keys. Store variant, seed, and answer order so paused rounds resume identically; preserve drill IDs and variant meaning when extending the bank.
- Mixed rounds contain up to six distinct drill families, prioritising due and new methods. Failed or assisted checks re-enter the review queue; retries switch scenario. Do not replace an unfinished round without the learner explicitly ending it.
- Spaced recall advances only on independent correct checks at least 24 hours apart; same-day retries must not extend the review date or inflate the level. A spaced-recall marker also requires success in both contexts. This is not a mastery certificate or consulting-readiness score.
- Reading a method during an unfinished round marks matching unanswered checks as assisted. Guidance after checking does not retroactively change a score. Framework progress stays local.

# Case maths lessons

- Cases now opens on the Overview dashboard (`src/Overview.jsx`, `/cases` and `/cases#overview`): a status line, next-step card, module tiles, a weekly plan, mastery by category, and suggestions. Section links use `/cases#overview`, `/cases#case-maths`, `/cases#frameworks`, and `/cases#full-cases`; native history and direct loads must select the corresponding section. Both learning modules stay mounted after opening to preserve in-memory progress when storage is blocked.
- `src/study-plan.js` keeps the weekly plan in `case-prep-plan-v1`: editable targets (cases, drills, cards) plus a 90-day local activity log keyed by local date. `recordActivity` is called once per completed case (Cases.jsx), checked drill (Frameworks.jsx), graded card (Flashcards.jsx), and finished maths exercise (CaseMaths.jsx). It never throws; when storage is blocked an in-memory copy keeps the dashboard current. Weeks run Monday to Sunday, the streak counts consecutive active days ending today or yesterday.
- `src/case-maths.js` contains thirteen original lessons (the nine foundations and business/decision lessons plus break-even market share, price cuts and required volume, customer lifetime value, and valuing a growing cash stream) with internal research references, standard formulas, worked examples, and deterministic variant-based exercises. Each exercise checks Set up → Calculate → Interpret. Keep time periods, units, cost definitions, rounding instructions, and modelling assumptions explicit.
- `src/CaseMaths.jsx` shows a separate worked example before practice. The library ends with an "Estimation anchors" card (`src/estimation-anchors.js`): approximate 2026 India figures for guesstimates from the prep book appendix, in Case Vault's wording. Hide calculation choices until requested and reveal exercise feedback only after checking. Setup feedback deliberately supports later calculation: these are guided learning checks, not an independent mastery or readiness score.
- `src/case-maths-practice.js` uses only `case-prep-case-maths-v1`. Preserve answer order, selected choices, and the first checked answer across reloads. Formula help or reading the matching lesson marks remaining checks as guided. Never replace an unfinished practice without an explicit finish/end action.
- Preserve the completed-practice count when a later retry ends early. The revisit list uses the most recent mistakes, guidance, and unattempted checks; it is not a spaced-recall schedule. Framework drills retain their separate spaced-recall system.
- `npm test` includes `src/case-maths.test.js`, with independent calculations across 240 variants per lesson for all thirteen lessons, answer locking, assistance, completion preservation, and storage isolation/recovery checks. It also includes `src/study-plan.test.js`, covering Monday week boundaries, streaks, 90-day pruning, target clamping, malformed storage, storage isolation, and blocked-storage recording. No backend or dependency changes are needed for this feature.
- The main header and Cases section navigation are sticky. Keep focus targets and scenario scrolling clear of both bars on small screens; interactive controls retain visible keyboard focus and 44px touch targets.

# Sector notes and flashcards

- `src/industries.js` combines 34 sector notes (`src/industries-2026*.js`), one per industry report in the IIMA Consult Prep Book 2026-27 (pp. 410-477). Each note has `summary`, `overview`, `segments`, `valueChain`, `drivers`, `kpis`, `forces`, `pestel`, `growth`, `challenges`, `models`, `facts`, `news`, `terms`, `caseIds`, plus `editions` and `source` for provenance. Write every line in Case Vault's own words: keep the report's facts and figures exactly, never copy its sentences (no run of 8+ words may match the source), and never add facts, figures or opinions it does not contain. Figures are dated context and the page says so. `caseIds` must resolve to real cases.
- `src/Industries.jsx` is the Industries tab (`/cases#industries`): search, tag chips, a card grid with read ticks and an "x of 34 read" bar, and a note page with sticky jump links. Opening a note calls `markIndustryRead` (`src/industry-progress.js`, key `case-prep-industries-v1`), which records the first read, latest read and visit count per note.
- `/cases#cards` is the flashcards section (`src/Flashcards.jsx`, rendered inside Cases), with thirteen decks in `src/flashcards.js`: consulting language, accounting, corporate finance, trading, statistics, case maths, economics, operations, customers, GRE vocabulary, sector terms, frameworks, strategy concepts. Every card needs a term, a plain-words definition (60 characters or more, 30 for vocabulary), and the trap that catches people. Vocabulary cards also need a part of speech in `short`, a sentence in `example`, and the word each is confused with in `trap`; add an `example` wherever a number makes it concrete, and a `figure` key when a picture is faster. Keep 20 or more cards per deck. Diagrams live in `src/CardFigure.jsx` on a 200x104 grid using the palette tokens, and every key there must be used by a card.
- `src/sound.js` synthesises short tones through the Web Audio API: no audio files, no network. It is on by default, toggled on the Cards and Mental maths sections, stored in `case-prep-sound-v1`, and every call is wrapped so a missing AudioContext or blocked storage never throws.
- Answers are always checked against the card (multiple choice, both directions), never self-rated. The learner then chooses when it returns: Again and Hard requeue it inside the round after the chosen minutes (`againMinutes`, 1/5/10); Good and Easy advance the level and schedule 1, 3, 7 or 14 days. Shuffle swaps due-first ordering for random. State lives in `case-prep-flashcards-v1`, separate from every other key.

# Interview guide

- `/cases#interview` (`src/InterviewGuide.jsx`, data in `src/interview-guide.js`) covers the whole MBB interview beyond the case: motivation, personal experience, case questions and the candidate's own questions, with what each part tests, how to answer, and a 21-item rehearsal checklist. The guidance is general material from Robert Mellon's *The Case Interview Workbook* introduction, used with the owner's permission for non-case content and written in Case Vault's own words. Never adapt that book's 60 cases.
- Checklist ticks live in `case-prep-interview-v1` (`src/interview-progress.js`), one record per item with the latest change; an untick is stored as `done: false`. The Overview shows an Interview tile and Settings an "Interview prep" line.

# Settings
